# TC-05 — Deterministic dispatch integration

TC-05 registers one operational deterministic capability: `CODE_EXECUTOR` operation `PREPARE_CANDIDATE`, contract `slice002-preparation-contract-v1`, rules `tc04-preparation-rules-v1`, and its exact proven scope. Registry selection is deterministic and controlled by Control Plane.

Eligibility distinguishes a legitimate unsupported task (`NOT_APPLICABLE`) from corruption, contradiction, stale claimed versions, authority violations, or failed oracles (`MATERIAL_BLOCKER`). Only `NOT_APPLICABLE` may leave an already-authorized normal path selectable. A blocker stops its scope and never falls back to an LLM.

Successful execution uses the strict existing `ExecutionResult`, verifies and ingests it through the ledger, records deterministic telemetry with zero model calls, and stops at `ADVISOR_HANDOFF_READY`. It never proposes a destination or marks Advisor review complete.

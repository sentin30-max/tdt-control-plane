# TC-03 — Offline projection and equivalence gates

TC-03 builds non-authoritative, non-operational, non-dispatchable projections from the immutable TC-02 manifests. It performs no model call and cannot alter role prompts, routing, authority, or result semantics.

The builder uses positive selection. Every unit remains inline unless a versioned exact or structural duplicate rule can reconstruct it from a same-authority surviving representation and an equality oracle passes. Protective material, adverse findings, blockers, unknown material, unresolved contract evidence, references without an authorized resolver, and identity fields remain inline.

Acceptance is offline only. It requires EG-01 through EG-12 to pass for identity, authority, protection, adverse findings, contracts, evidence, state, blockers, scope, traceability, reversibility, and no silent correction. Exact reconstruction is the final oracle. An accepted projection is not authorized for dispatch.

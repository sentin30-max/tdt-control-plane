# TC-04 — Deterministic preparation shadow

TC-04 evaluates whether the historical `CODE_EXECUTOR_PREPARATION` operation can be reproduced by a pure local transformation. It does not replace the operational executor, dispatch a result, alter routing, or bypass Advisor review.

The preparer accepts only the versioned Slice 002 preparation contract. It copies the historical request, applies the eight declared field updates, applies the four exact literal substitutions in order, and emits the existing strict `ExecutionResult` envelope. Every material output field maps to a declared rule. An independent invariant oracle checks preservation, exact updates, identity, authority boundaries, and absence of effects or routing.

Unknown versions, malformed or ambiguous inputs, unexpected fields, identity collisions, changed authority or protected baselines, contradictions, missing inputs, and oracle failures stop with `DETERMINISTIC_PREPARATION_NOT_APPLICABLE`. All persistence and recovery tests are shadow-only.
